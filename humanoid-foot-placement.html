Ongoing project

Foot placement for humanoid locomotion

Developing a PPO-based framework for precise foot placement and alternating stepping with the Unitree G1. The task combines commanded foot positions and yaw angles with stance-foot stability, supporting straight walking and turning.

Reinforcement learningPPOHumanoid locomotionIsaac Sim
Training environment: 23 actuated degrees of freedom and 512 parallel simulation environments, built on ASAP / HumanoidVerse.
My contributions
  • Designed a foot-placement environment for tracking commanded 3D positions and yaw angles while stabilizing the stance foot.
  • Developed a gait state machine with an initial standing phase, target-based foot switching, and timeout handling.
  • Implemented curriculum learning and target generators with randomized step length, width, and heading, plus fixed-step circular turning trajectories.
  • Designed heading-frame actor–critic observations with commands, tracking errors, contacts, and swing-foot identity, with privileged base velocity for the critic.
  • Customized rewards for placement accuracy, upper-body stability, and controlled roll/pitch while permitting yaw rotation.
  • Integrated parallel PPO training and policy evaluation in Isaac Sim.
