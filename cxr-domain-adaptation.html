Domain adaptation for pediatric chest X-ray classification

Investigated transfer from adult to pediatric chest X-rays through a team comparison of a CLIP-style dual encoder, domain-adversarial neural networks (DANN), and supervised contrastive learning (SupCon), alongside a pediatric-only baseline.

Domain adaptationContrastive learningDANNSupCon

My contributions: Implemented DANN and SupCon, conducted model training, and co-wrote the report. Evaluated classification performance across pediatric training-set sizes and examined feature representations using t-SNE.

Experiments explored the effects of limited labeled data and class imbalance. SupCon achieved an F1 score of 0.9238 in the 1,024-sample setting.
