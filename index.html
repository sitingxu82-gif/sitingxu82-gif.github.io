Differentiable simulation for soft robotic fish

Developed simulation tools for binary material optimization, residual physics learning, and hydrodynamic modeling. This work explored how material assignments and physical models affect the motion of actuated deformable systems.

Differentiable physicsSoft roboticsMaterial optimizationC++ / DiffPD
Representative result: Reduced trajectory error from 0.233 mm to 0.041 mm in a simulation-to-simulation residual learning test; experimental trajectories were evaluated separately.
Explore my contributions
  • Extended the DiffPD C++ core with heterogeneous element-wise materials, including elastic energy, force, and force differential computations.
  • Built a binary soft/stiff material optimization pipeline using surrogate gradients, evaluating layouts that maximize actuated-beam tip oscillation amplitude.
  • Compared DiffPD and Newton solvers on cantilever beams across time-step sizes and material stiffnesses.
  • Refined residual physics learning with curriculum learning and modified loss functions.
  • Implemented Lighthill-inspired added-mass and resistive-force models, and calibrated drag/thrust coefficients against a simulation baseline at five frequencies spanning 2–18 Hz.

Ongoing project

Foot placement for humanoid locomotion

Developing a PPO-based framework for precise foot placement and alternating stepping with the Unitree G1. The task combines commanded foot positions and yaw angles with stance-foot stability, supporting straight walking and turning.

Reinforcement learningPPOHumanoid locomotionIsaac Sim
Training environment: 23 actuated degrees of freedom and 512 parallel simulation environments, built on ASAP / HumanoidVerse.
Explore my contributions
  • Designed a foot-placement environment for tracking commanded 3D positions and yaw angles while stabilizing the stance foot.
  • Developed a gait state machine with an initial standing phase, target-based foot switching, and timeout handling.
  • Implemented curriculum learning and target generators with randomized step length, width, and heading, plus fixed-step circular turning trajectories.
  • Designed heading-frame actor–critic observations with commands, tracking errors, contacts, and swing-foot identity, with privileged base velocity for the critic.
  • Customized rewards for placement accuracy, upper-body stability, and controlled roll/pitch while permitting yaw rotation.
  • Integrated parallel PPO training and policy evaluation in Isaac Sim.

Digital twin for marine power systems

Developed a digital twin client for a single-cylinder engine, combining sensor acquisition, computational modeling, and a Qt interface for real-time monitoring. Integrated hardware and software to connect measured engine behavior with predicted thermodynamic states.

Digital twinsSensor integrationNSGA-IIQt development
Model calibration: Achieved 3.92% average peak-pressure prediction error relative to measured results by automatically calibrating 33 model parameters.
Explore my contributions
  • Integrated sensors with an industrial control computer for real-time data collection.
  • Developed a Qt client with monitoring and control functions, including real-time in-cylinder pressure curves.
  • Built a thermodynamic model and integrated it into the client.
  • Implemented port communication and transmission protocols for robust sensor data flow.
  • Developed NSGA-II-based automatic calibration of 33 model parameters.
  • Contributed geometric and physical models and a fault sample library for diagnosis.

Domain adaptation for pediatric chest X-ray classification

Investigated transfer from adult to pediatric chest X-rays through a team comparison of a CLIP-style dual encoder, domain-adversarial neural networks (DANN), and supervised contrastive learning (SupCon), alongside a pediatric-only baseline.

Domain adaptationContrastive learningDANNSupCon

My contributions: Implemented DANN and SupCon, conducted model training, and co-wrote the report. Evaluated classification performance across pediatric training-set sizes and examined feature representations using t-SNE.

Experiments explored the effects of limited labeled data and class imbalance. SupCon achieved an F1 score of 0.9238 in the 1,024-sample setting.
